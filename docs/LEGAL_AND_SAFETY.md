# Legal, Safety and Responsibility Notice

Royal Cyber Lab is provided for education, awareness, and defensive security practice. The examples are designed for systems that you own, administer, or have explicit permission to test.

**Important:** A statement such as “educational purpose”, “demo”, or “at your own risk” does not make an unauthorized activity lawful and does not remove a user's or developer's legal responsibilities. Laws vary by location. You are responsible for understanding and following applicable laws, contracts, and organizational policies.

## Never use this material to
- Access, scan, disrupt, damage, or modify another person's device, account, website, or network without permission.
- Steal credentials, intercept private communications, deploy malware, evade detection, or maintain unauthorized access.
- Perform denial-of-service, destructive, deceptive, or privacy-invasive actions.

## Approved practice environments
- Your own Kali Linux virtual machine.
- A deliberately isolated local lab with synthetic targets.
- A training platform whose rules explicitly authorize the exercise.
- A system covered by clear, current, written authorization and a defined scope.

## If an exercise behaves unexpectedly
Stop the activity, disconnect the lab network if needed, preserve relevant notes, and ask the system owner or instructor before continuing.

The maintainers provide no guarantee that every command is suitable for every environment. Review commands before running them. Do not run scripts with elevated privileges unless you understand why they need them.
