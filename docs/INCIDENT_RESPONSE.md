# Incident Response Quick Guide

## 1. Prepare
Know who owns the system, where backups are, and how to contact the responsible administrator.

## 2. Identify
Record what was observed, when it began, affected assets, and confidence level. Avoid speculation.

## 3. Contain
Follow the organization's incident plan. Do not power off or modify a system unless the responsible team directs you; volatile evidence may be lost.

## 4. Eradicate and recover
Only authorized responders should remove malicious artifacts, rotate credentials, restore clean backups, and validate the system.

## 5. Learn
Write a factual timeline, document root causes, and track corrective actions.

Never upload sensitive logs or secrets to this public repository.
